Role

Tools

Problem

Turning fan-character artwork into a physical plush product needed a factory, quality control, and a fulfillment path to buyers without the products passing through the designer's own hands, while minimizing unsold-inventory risk.

What I Built

  • Ran prepaid, made-to-order sales through Witchform sized to confirmed orders
  • Sourced and negotiated with a Taobao manufacturer and reviewed samples before mass production
  • Connected factory, freight forwarder, inspection service, and domestic carrier into a direct-to-buyer fulfillment workflow
  • Centralized buyer Q&A through Peing

Result

₩10M total revenue, 235 account followers, 1,000+ cumulative reposts (as of August 24, 2026).
