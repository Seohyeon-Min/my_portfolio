Poseidon Skate

Role

Technical Art · Shader Development · Production

Tools

Unity URP, HLSL, C#, Blender, Perforce, Jira

Problem

A three-week team project needed a rideable procedural ocean, combat-feel VFX/camera work, and a character model, plus schedule/priority management across the team.

What I Built

  • Wrote reusable ocean, wave, and tornado HLSL shaders
  • Built a rideable wave as generated mesh geometry with its own collider, separate from the GPU water shader
  • Authored VFX and camera effects for combat feel, and modeled/rigged a low-poly Poseidon in Blender
  • Managed schedule and priorities in Jira, with Perforce for source control

Result

Team finished core gameplay first, leaving time for the art/shader polish above — shipped as a playable three-week team build.
