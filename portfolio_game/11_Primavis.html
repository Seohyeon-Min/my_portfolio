Authorship, stated explicitly: I set the art direction and visual targets and made the look and performance decisions. I modeled the characters and creatures (the child, the bird and the animals); their animation was generated with AI. Shader and rendering code was implemented with AI assistance under my direction. Background props (trees, flowers): Tommy Ji. Game design: Seungju Song. Programming: Seungju Song, Sam Jeon. Sound: Port Nugent.

Role

Art Direction · Programming · Production (credited in the game's ending credits)

Event

thatgamecompany game jam, 2026 · two weeks · team of five

Tools

Unity 6 URP, HLSL, C#, FMOD, WebGL

What I did

  • Set a watercolor / gouache / pastel art direction where winter visibly retreats in steps until spring arrives.
  • Measured a full-screen Anisotropic Kuwahara watercolor filter (about 14 ms at 1080p) against material-based watercolor plus a 3x3 bilateral blur (0.135 ms), and chose the material approach for the web build.
  • Season change shown as a ring wipe that repaints trees, flowers and grass outward from the child.
  • Web-build stalls: renderers cut from 75,755 to 40,272; first-use shader compiles (up to about 690 ms in one frame) moved into the game's first frames by drawing all 141 materials once.
