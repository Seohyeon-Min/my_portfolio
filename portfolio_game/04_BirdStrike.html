Bird Strike

Role

Gameplay Programmer · Game Designer · Artist · Production Lead

Tools

C++, Clip Studio Paint, Cakewalk, raylib, GitHub

Problem

First university project (freshman year): build a rhythm-action game in C++ without a commercial engine, in a two-month scope, where difficulty needed to escalate from player skill rather than a fixed spawn rate.

What I Built

  • Audio-timeline beat detection with rhythm-synchronized crow spawning
  • Dynamic attack-rate subdivision (up to 4x) triggered by chained targets
  • Two-stage difficulty escalation with a leaderboard and achievements
  • Original concept art, character design, logo, and audio

Result

Completed and shipped within the two-month scope as sole programmer, designer, and artist on the team.
