Sushi Dojo

Authorship, stated explicitly: the game's image assets (dishes, ingredients, title, UI panels, backgrounds) were generated with AI under my art direction — I defined the style, selected and edited the results, and integrated them. My code was written with AI assistance. Networking, match flow, recipes and balance were written by teammates Daniel Shin and Lance.

Role

Art Direction · Visual Implementation · Trailer

Event

DubHacks 2026 (University of Washington) · 36-hour hackathon · team of three

Tools

Unity URP (2D Renderer), HLSL, C#, After Effects

What I did

  • Locked one faceted, low-poly illustration style and a warm sushi-bar palette so about 40 images made in one day read as one game.
  • Built the presentation: spotlit title table, cloche reveals, ingredient card hand, speech-bubble scores, dust-puff cooking, leaf transitions.
  • Small shaders to make 3D props sit under Unity's 2D Renderer: fake-lit low-poly props, toon cloud puffs, soft fog, cross sparkles, full-screen pixelation.
  • Throwable ingredients, chopping-board physics and a jelly-bounce chef; cut the one-minute trailer in After Effects.
