Project Toon

Toon Shader

Project Overview

Built three toon-lighting approaches that quantize illumination into stylized, cel-shaded bands.

Tasks Completed

  • Transformed normals correctly for lighting calculations
  • Implemented custom-range lighting bands
  • Implemented uniformly spaced lighting bands
  • Implemented gradient-texture ramp shading

Reflection

This study connected my interest in stylized rendering with practical shader work. The most challenging part was understanding fwidth; applying it successfully taught me how screen-space derivatives can soften aliasing at lighting-band boundaries.
